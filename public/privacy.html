CAREEROS PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Operator details are required before commercial launch. The operator must add its legal entity name, address and a monitored privacy/support contact to this policy before accepting customer accounts. This draft describes the implemented product flows and should be reviewed against the final deployment and applicable law.

Information processed

CareerOS can process your account identifier and email as supplied by the authentication service; career profile and goals; roadmaps and task status; AI conversations; resume text you paste; job descriptions; interview answers and feedback; learning progress; DSA attempt notes; job applications; subscription status; and basic activity records for product operation and security.

Purpose and use

Data is used to provide career planning, persist progress, generate requested AI assistance, track learning and job applications, enforce usage limits, support account data export/deletion, and protect the service. CareerOS does not claim that all recommendations are verified facts.

AI processing

When you invoke an AI feature, relevant text and profile context may be sent to the configured AI service provider to produce the requested result. Do not submit information you do not have permission to share. AI output may be wrong and should be reviewed before you rely on it. API credentials are intended to remain server-side.

Resumes and job data

Resume Studio currently accepts pasted text. It does not extract PDF/DOCX files in this release. Saved resume and job description content is private to the signed-in account through the application API. Keep sensitive personal data to a minimum.

Sharing and third parties

Hosting, database, authentication and configured AI services process information needed to operate the product. No employer, recruiter, or job board is represented as connected unless a real integration is configured. The current release does not execute arbitrary submitted code, connect to private recruiter systems, or make hiring decisions.

Retention, export and deletion

You can export CareerOS application records in JSON from Settings and request permanent deletion of those records. Application-data deletion does not delete the underlying platform authentication account; that account has a separate management process. Backups or security records may be subject to platform-level retention outside the application’s direct control.

Security

CareerOS APIs are designed to scope private records by authenticated user. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The operator should configure provider secrets securely, review logs and access, manage backups, and publish an incident response and retention policy before production launch.

Your rights and contact

For access, correction, export, deletion, or privacy questions, contact the operator using the privacy contact published in the final production version. Operator legal name, registered address, privacy/grievance contact, and applicable retention periods must be filled in by the operator before public commercial launch.
