GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
No career guarantees.

CareerOS is designed to help you plan and prepare. You remain the decision-maker at every step.

?

Still figuring out your next move?

Start with a profile and one career goal. You can add more information and tools as you go.

Does CareerOS guarantee a job or interview?

No. Career outcomes depend on many factors. CareerOS offers planning, practice and feedback; it does not guarantee employment, salary, interview selection or promotion.

Will it make up resume achievements for me?

No. The coaching experience should preserve factual integrity. Suggestions need your confirmation and any metrics or achievements must reflect your real experience.

Can I upload my PDF or DOCX resume?

The initial release supports pasted resume text. Direct PDF/DOCX extraction is not enabled in this release, so copy the text into Resume Studio.

Does CareerOS execute code?

No. The DSA module tracks attempts and links to practice sources. Sandboxed code execution is not enabled in this release.

Is AI advice always correct?

No. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Verify important claims and treat evaluations as coaching guidance, not a hiring decision or accredited assessment.

What happens to my career data?

CareerOS stores profile, roadmap, interview, resume and job-search data in your account. You can export or delete CareerOS application data from Settings. Platform authentication-account deletion is a separate action.
